==============================
WINDOW FUNCTIONS
==============================

Perform calculations across a set of table rows related to the current row without collapsing them into a single output.

Window functions are initiated with the OVER clause, which defines how the rows are partitioned and ordered.

1. Example:
SELECT depname, empno, salary, avg(salary) OVER (PARTITION BY depname)
FROM empsalary;

depname | empno | salary | avg
-----------+-------+--------+-----------------------
develop | 11 | 5200 | 5020.0000000000000000
develop | 7 | 4200 | 5020.0000000000000000
develop | 9 | 4500 | 5020.0000000000000000
develop | 8 | 6000 | 5020.0000000000000000
develop | 10 | 5200 | 5020.0000000000000000
personnel | 5 | 3500 | 3700.0000000000000000
personnel | 2 | 3900 | 3700.0000000000000000
sales | 3 | 4800 | 4866.6666666666666667
sales | 1 | 5000 | 4866.6666666666666667
sales | 4 | 4800 | 4866.6666666666666667
(10 rows)

- The OVER clause defines the window of rows.
- PARTITION BY divides the result set into partitions.
- ORDER BY determines the order of rows within partitions.

2. Ranking Example:
SELECT depname, empno, salary, rank() OVER (PARTITION BY depname ORDER BY salary DESC)
FROM empsalary;

depname | empno | salary | rank
-----------+-------+--------+------
develop | 8 | 6000 | 1
develop | 10 | 5200 | 2
develop | 11 | 5200 | 2
develop | 9 | 4500 | 4
develop | 7 | 4200 | 5
personnel | 2 | 3900 | 1
personnel | 5 | 3500 | 2
sales | 1 | 5000 | 1
sales | 4 | 4800 | 2
sales | 3 | 4800 | 2
(10 rows)

- Rank employees within each department based on salary.

3. Cumulative Sum Example:
SELECT salary, sum(salary) OVER (ORDER BY salary) 
FROM empsalary;

salary | sum
--------+-------
3500 | 3500
3900 | 7400
4200 | 11600
4500 | 16100
4800 | 25700
4800 | 25700
5000 | 30700
5200 | 41100
5200 | 41100
6000 | 47100
(10 rows)

- Calculate a running total (cumulative sum) of salaries from lowest to highest.

- Window functions execute after WHERE, GROUP BY, and HAVING clauses, so they can't be used directly in those clauses.
- Subselects can be used to filter results after window functions are applied.
